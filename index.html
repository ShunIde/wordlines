    How to read the trees

    Inherited

    Passed down from parent language to child, like Latin vīnum → Spanish vino. Words that share an ancestor this way are cognates.

    Borrowed

    Taken from another language, often an unrelated one. Chinese 蜜 came from Tocharian even though Chinese isn't Indo-European.

    Uncertain

    Clearly connected, but scholars disagree on which language borrowed from which.
